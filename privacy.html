CleanPhotoFlick

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2, 2026

1. Your Photos

CleanPhotoFlick requires permission to access your photo library to provide its photo organization and cleanup features.

Your photos are processed locally on your device. We do not upload your photo library to our servers.

2. Photo Deletion

Photos marked for deletion are placed into a review queue.

Photos are not deleted simply because you swipe left. You must review and confirm your selections.

3. Free Usage and Pro Purchases

CleanPhotoFlick provides 150 free photo swipes. Unlimited photo swipes can be unlocked through a one-time in-app purchase.

Purchase transactions are processed through Apple's App Store and StoreKit services.

We do not directly collect or store your payment card information.

4. Local Application Data

CleanPhotoFlick stores application preferences, photo review progress, usage counters and purchase-related status as needed to operate the application.

5. Advertising

CleanPhotoFlick does not display third-party advertisements.

6. Photo Permissions

You can manage or revoke photo library permissions through your device's iOS Settings.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when application features or privacy practices change.
