Privacy Policy

CleanPhotoFlick
Effective: September 20, 2026

Privacy First

CleanPhotoFlick is designed to help you review and clean up your personal photo library while keeping your photos private.

CleanPhotoFlick does not require an account and does not upload your photo library to our servers.

Photo Library Access

CleanPhotoFlick requests access to your Apple Photos library so it can display photos for you to review and allow you to select photos for deletion.

You may grant Full Access or Limited Access using Apple's photo permission controls.

Your photos are accessed using Apple's Photos framework and are processed on your device.

iCloud Photos

If you use iCloud Photos, some photos may not be stored locally on your device. When necessary, iOS may retrieve those photos from iCloud through Apple's Photos framework so they can be displayed inside CleanPhotoFlick.

This is handled by Apple's Photos and iCloud services. CleanPhotoFlick does not upload those photos to a CleanPhotoFlick server.

Deleting Photos

Swiping left on a photo does not immediately delete it. CleanPhotoFlick first places the photo into a deletion queue.

You can review the queue and remove photos from it before requesting deletion.

When you choose to delete the selected photos, CleanPhotoFlick requests the deletion through Apple's Photos framework. iOS may display an additional system confirmation before the deletion is completed.

Deleted photos may remain in Apple's Recently Deleted album for a period of time according to Apple's Photos behavior and settings.

Data Collection

CleanPhotoFlick does not require you to create an account.

CleanPhotoFlick does not use its own systems to collect:

  • Your photos or videos
  • Your name or contact information
  • Your precise location
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Browsing history
  • Payment information

Local App Data

CleanPhotoFlick may store app preferences, progress, statistics, trophy progress, and information needed to maintain your current cleanup session locally on your device.

This information is used to provide app functionality and is not uploaded to our servers.

Analytics and Advertising

CleanPhotoFlick does not include third-party advertising and does not use your photo library for advertising purposes.

Third-Party Services

CleanPhotoFlick relies on Apple system services such as Photos and, when applicable, iCloud Photos. Your use of Apple services is also subject to Apple's own privacy policies and terms.

Children's Privacy

CleanPhotoFlick does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app does not require an account or request personal information from users.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if CleanPhotoFlick's features or privacy practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will identify the latest version.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or CleanPhotoFlick, please visit the CleanPhotoFlick Support page.
