Getting Started

CleanPhotoFlick makes cleaning your photo library easy.

  1. Allow access to your photo library.
  2. Swipe right to keep a photo.
  3. Swipe left to mark a photo for deletion.
  4. Review your Delete Queue.
  5. Confirm which photos you want to delete.

Nothing is deleted simply by swiping left.

Free Version

Every user receives 150 free photo swipes.

Both left and right swipes count toward the free allowance.

Once your allowance is exhausted, you can purchase Pro to continue swiping.

Restore Purchases

If you have previously purchased Pro, you can restore your purchase.

  1. Open CleanPhotoFlick.
  2. Navigate to Settings.
  3. Select Restore Purchases.
  4. Follow any Apple verification prompts.

Use the same Apple Account associated with your original purchase.

Photo Privacy

Your photos are processed locally on your device. CleanPhotoFlick does not upload your photo library to our servers.
