CleanPhotoFlick Help

How to use CleanPhotoFlick and get support.

How to Use CleanPhotoFlick

CleanPhotoFlick gives you a quick way to work through your photo library whenever you have a few spare minutes.

1
Open CleanPhotoFlick

On your first launch, you'll see a short introduction explaining how CleanPhotoFlick works.

2
Allow Photo Access

CleanPhotoFlick needs access to your Photos library to display photos for review.

3
Start Flicking

CleanPhotoFlick presents photos one at a time. Decide whether you want to keep each photo or mark it for deletion.

4
Review Your Delete Queue

Photos marked for deletion remain in a review queue until you decide what to do with them.

5
Verify and Delete

Review your selections carefully. When you're ready, request deletion and approve Apple's system confirmation.

Flick Controls

← Swipe Left
Mark the current photo for deletion.
Swipe Right →
Keep the current photo.
Undo
Reverse your most recent swipe.
Tap Photo
Open the photo in a larger preview.

Don't like swiping? You can use the Delete and Keep buttons underneath the photo instead.

Nothing Is Deleted When You Swipe

Swiping left does not immediately delete a photo.

A left swipe only adds the photo to CleanPhotoFlick's deletion queue.

The number near the top of the Flick screen shows how many photos are currently waiting in your deletion queue.

Tap that indicator whenever you want to review the photos you've selected.

How to Review & Delete Photos

1
Open Verify Deletes

Tap the delete-queue indicator at the top of the Flick screen.

2
Review Every Photo

Look through the photos you've marked for deletion.

3
Changed Your Mind?

Tap the X on a photo to remove it from the delete queue and keep it.

4
Delete the Remaining Photos

Tap the Delete Photos button when you're satisfied with your selections.

5
Confirm

CleanPhotoFlick asks you to confirm your request. Apple Photos may then display its own system confirmation before anything is removed.

Viewing a Photo Before Deciding

Need a better look before deciding?

Tap the displayed photo to open the larger photo preview. You can zoom in to inspect details before deciding whether to keep the photo or mark it for deletion.

Undoing Your Last Flick

If you accidentally swipe the wrong direction, tap the Undo button between Delete and Keep.

CleanPhotoFlick will return you to the previous photo so you can make another choice.

Full vs. Limited Photo Access

Full Access

Allows CleanPhotoFlick to work with the photos available in your Photos library.

Limited Access

CleanPhotoFlick will only be able to display photos you've specifically allowed it to access.

You can allow access to additional photos from CleanPhotoFlick Settings or change the app's photo permissions through iPhone Settings.

Photos Stored in iCloud

If you use iCloud Photos and Optimize iPhone Storage, some full-resolution photos may not currently be stored on your iPhone.

iOS may need to retrieve a photo from iCloud before CleanPhotoFlick can display it.

If a photo isn't loading, make sure you have an internet connection and try loading the photo again.

Photo Deck & Shuffle

CleanPhotoFlick creates a randomized deck from the photos currently available to the app.

Work through the deck at your own pace. When a pass is complete, you can start another shuffled pass.

Your current delete queue is kept when you start another pass unless you choose to clear it.

Trophies

CleanPhotoFlick includes optional trophies for reaching cleanup milestones.

You can earn trophies for things such as:

  • Reviewing photos
  • Deleting verified photos
  • Spending time cleaning your library
  • Reaching estimated storage milestones

Don't want trophies or achievement popups?

Open Settings → Enable Trophies and turn the option off.

Your existing progress is kept if trophies are disabled.

Lifetime Statistics

CleanPhotoFlick keeps local statistics to show your cleanup progress, including:

  • Photos reviewed
  • Photos deleted
  • Time spent using CleanPhotoFlick
  • Estimated storage saved

These statistics are stored locally for app functionality.

Estimated Storage Saved

Storage-saved values displayed by CleanPhotoFlick are estimates.

Actual storage usage varies depending on HEIC/JPEG compression, Live Photos, iCloud optimization, photo edits and other Apple Photos storage behavior.

Photos placed in Apple's Recently Deleted album may also continue using storage until permanently removed.

I Accidentally Deleted a Photo

Apple Photos normally places deleted photos into its Recently Deleted album for a period of time.

Open the Apple Photos app and locate the Recently Deleted album to see whether your photo can still be recovered.

Recovery availability and retention are controlled by Apple, not CleanPhotoFlick.

Troubleshooting

No Photos Appear

Check that CleanPhotoFlick has permission to access your Photos library. If you're using Limited Access, make sure you've selected photos for the app to access.

A Photo Won't Load

The photo may be stored in iCloud. Check your internet connection and try again.

I Can't Delete a Photo

Verify that CleanPhotoFlick has read/write access to Photos and that the photo is still available in your library.

My Library Changed While Using the App

CleanPhotoFlick monitors the Photos library for changes. Photos deleted or changed elsewhere may no longer be available in your current session.

Your Privacy

CleanPhotoFlick does not require an account and does not upload your photo library to our servers.

Photo selection and cleanup are performed using Apple's Photos framework on your device.

Read the complete CleanPhotoFlick Privacy Policy for more information.
