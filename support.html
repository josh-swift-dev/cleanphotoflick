CleanPhotoFlick Support

Quick photo cleanup, one flick at a time.

Getting Started

  1. Open CleanPhotoFlick.
  2. Complete the introductory screens.
  3. Allow access to your Photos library.
  4. CleanPhotoFlick will prepare your photo deck.
  5. Review each photo and choose whether to keep it or mark it for deletion.

Using CleanPhotoFlick

Swipe Right
Keep the photo.
Swipe Left
Add the photo to your deletion queue.
Undo
Reverse your most recent swipe.
Tap Photo
Open a larger preview of the photo.

You can also use the Delete and Keep buttons underneath the photo instead of swiping.

Deleting Photos

Swiping left does not immediately delete a photo.

Photos you mark for deletion are placed into your deletion queue. The number of photos waiting for review appears near the top of the Flick screen.

When you're ready:

  1. Open the deletion queue.
  2. Review the photos you selected.
  3. Tap the X on any photo you want to keep.
  4. Tap the Delete Photos button when you're satisfied.
  5. Confirm the deletion request.
  6. Approve Apple's system confirmation if it appears.

I Deleted a Photo by Mistake

Deleted photos may be moved to the Recently Deleted album in the Apple Photos app.

Open Photos → Recently Deleted to see whether the photo can still be recovered.

Recovery availability and retention periods are controlled by Apple Photos.

Photo Access

CleanPhotoFlick requires access to your Photos library to display photos and request deletion of photos you select.

Limited Access

If you choose Limited Access, CleanPhotoFlick will only display photos you've allowed it to access.

You can allow additional photos from CleanPhotoFlick's Settings screen or change photo permissions in iPhone Settings.

A Photo Is Taking a While to Load

If you use iCloud Photos with optimized iPhone storage, the original photo may need to be downloaded by iOS before it can be displayed.

Make sure your device has an internet connection and try loading the photo again.

Trophies

CleanPhotoFlick includes optional trophies for reaching cleanup milestones such as reviewing photos, deleting verified photos, and spending time cleaning your library.

Trophy notifications and the Trophies tab can be disabled from:

CleanPhotoFlick → Settings → Enable Trophies

Turning trophies off does not erase your existing progress.

Storage Saved

Storage-saved values shown by CleanPhotoFlick are estimates.

Actual storage recovered can vary because of iCloud Photos, optimized storage, HEIC compression, Live Photos, edits, and other Apple Photos storage behavior.

Privacy

CleanPhotoFlick does not require an account and does not upload your photo library to our servers.

Photo selection and cleanup are performed using Apple's Photos framework on your device.

For additional information, read our Privacy Policy.

Troubleshooting

No photos are appearing

Verify that CleanPhotoFlick has permission to access your Photos library. If you selected Limited Access, make sure at least one photo has been made available to the app.

The app cannot delete a photo

Verify that CleanPhotoFlick has read/write Photos permission and that the photo is still available in your library.

My photo library changed

CleanPhotoFlick responds to changes made to the Apple Photos library. Photos deleted or modified elsewhere may no longer be available in your current cleanup session.
